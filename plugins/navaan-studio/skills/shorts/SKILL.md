# Shorts and reels

1. Use `final_reel_cap.mp4` only. It is 1 minute or less, which makes YouTube treat it as a Short.
2. Title format: native script title, then language and style, then `#Shorts`. Example: `चाँद बनकर आ जाओ | Hindi Lo-fi Love Song #Shorts`.
3. Description: one line hook, hashtags. For Instagram captions leave out any AI or tool credit line, it is not required there. Add the full song link only after the full video is final.
4. Tags: language lo-fi song, language love song, new language song 2026, shorts, NAVAAN.
5. Disclosure: set the altered or synthetic content answer to Yes, because the music is AI generated. Not made for kids.
6. Take title, hashtags, playlist, credit text and the AI disclosure from `navaan.config.json`. Hand the file to the publisher agent. Publishing needs the recorded gate and the user's explicit yes.
